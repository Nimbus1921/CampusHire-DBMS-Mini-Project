Normalization Justification (1NF → 2NF → 3NF)
Academic Proof
First Normal Form (1NF)
  • Atomic Attributes: All attribute values are indivisible scalars (no comma-separated lists of values).
  • Elimination of Repeating Groups: Instead of storing eligible departments as a comma-separated string in job_openings, a separate associative table job_eligibility_departments was introduced.
  • Unique Row Identification: Each table possesses an explicit Primary Key (e.g., student_id, job_id).
Second Normal Form (2NF)
  • Prerequisite: The schema is strictly in 1NF.
  • No Partial Functional Dependency: In tables with composite keys (like job_eligibility_departments(job_id, department_id) and interview_rounds(application_id, round_number)), all non-prime attributes depend on the whole candidate key, not just a subset.
  • Entities like students and job_openings have single-attribute surrogate keys, naturally satisfying 2NF.
Third Normal Form (3NF)
  • Prerequisite: The schema is in 2NF.
  • No Transitive Dependency: If $X \to Y$ and $Y \to Z$, non-prime attributes must not depend on other non-prime attributes.
  • Example: Company details (company_name, industry) are stored in companies, NOT duplicated in job_openings. job_openings only stores company_id (FK).
  • Example: Department details are stored in departments, NOT duplicated in students.
